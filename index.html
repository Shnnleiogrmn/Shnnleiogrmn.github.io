DialyCare

Dialysis Scheduling & Notification Application

Smarter Scheduling, Timely Care.

A Dialysis Scheduling and Notification Application designed to improve the management of dialysis schedules and communication between dialysis-center staff, patients, and their designated relatives or companions.

Explore the project +

Problem

Dialysis patients require regular treatment schedules, but schedule and treatment updates are often communicated manually through calls and personal messages. This can require repeated communication and may cause delays or missed information between dialysis-center staff, patients, and their relatives or companions.

My role

Lead Backend Developer & DevOps / Deployment Specialist

Technologies

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Firebase
  • UniSMS
  • Windows desktop
  • Android / iOS

Key features

  • Patient profile and contact information management
  • Dialysis schedule and batch management
  • Automated SMS notifications
  • Patient session-status monitoring
  • Start-of-session notifications
  • End-of-session / pickup-related notifications
  • Dashboard for authorized dialysis-center personnel
  • User authentication and role-based access

Project overview +

Who it is for

People with difficulty speaking, including individuals with aphasia or communication difficulties following a stroke.

Interaction

Design focus
