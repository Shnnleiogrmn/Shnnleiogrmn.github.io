DialyCare

01 — CASE STUDY

Overview

DialyCare is a Dialysis Scheduling and Notification Application. It brings scheduling, session-status monitoring and SMS communication into a coordinated workflow for dialysis-center personnel, patients and their designated relatives or companions.

Status
Capstone · In Development
My role
Lead Backend Developer & DevOps / Deployment Specialist

02 — CASE STUDY

Problem

Regular dialysis treatment requires dependable scheduling and communication. Manual calls and personal messages can mean repeated follow-ups, delays or missed updates between staff, patients and companions.

03 — CASE STUDY

Approach & system

The project organizes patient information, treatment schedules and session updates. Authorized staff use a desktop application; patients and relatives use a mobile application.

Coordination workflow

  1. Patient scheduling
  2. Session monitoring
  3. Patient / relative updates
  4. SMS notifications
STAFF Desktop application
PATIENT / RELATIVE Mobile application
