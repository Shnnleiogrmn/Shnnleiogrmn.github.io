Research / Agricultural Technology

Development of an Automated Solar-Powered Subsurface Irrigation System for Eggplant Farming

Research Proposal

A proposed smart irrigation system combining solar energy, sensor-based monitoring, automated control and subsurface water delivery for eggplant farming.

01 Overview 02 Problem areas 03 Proposed approach & system 04 Research themes 05 Research process 06 Current status

01 — CASE STUDY

Overview

This COERES (Methods of Research) proposal explores an automated solar-powered subsurface irrigation system for eggplant farming. It combines renewable energy, soil and environmental monitoring, and automated irrigation decisions to deliver water to the plant root zone.

02 — CASE STUDY

Problem areas

  1. Over / under-irrigation
  2. Water waste
  3. Waterlogging
  4. Manual irrigation labor
  5. Clogging / flow issues
  6. Reliance on conventional energy

03 — CASE STUDY

Proposed approach & system

The concept uses environmental information to guide automated water delivery, supported by solar energy. It aims to address irrigation challenges; these objectives are not validated outcomes.

04 — CASE STUDY

Research themes

  • Solar Energy
  • Smart Irrigation
  • Automation
  • Subsurface Irrigation
  • Agricultural Technology
  • Sensor-Based Monitoring

The proposal does not establish a final controller, sensor model, pump specification or production architecture.

05 — CASE STUDY

Research process

The research process included on-site data gathering through a local-farmer interview and documentation involving the barangay office / municipal agricultural engineer.

06 — CASE STUDY

Current status

Research Proposal — COERES / Methods of Research. The system remains proposed; experimental outcomes have not been reported.
